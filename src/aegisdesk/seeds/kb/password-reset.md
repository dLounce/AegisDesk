# Password and Account Recovery

Self-service password reset is available to all active employees through the identity
portal. The reset link expires after fifteen minutes.

An account locks after ten consecutive failed sign-in attempts and unlocks automatically
after thirty minutes.

Support staff never ask an employee for their password, and never read a temporary password
aloud or type it into a ticket. Recovery is always completed by the employee through the
portal.

If the employee cannot receive the reset message, verify their registered contact method in
the identity portal rather than changing it on their behalf.
