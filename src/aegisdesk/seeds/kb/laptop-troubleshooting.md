# Laptop and Device Issues

Collect the asset tag before troubleshooting. It appears on the underside label and in the
endpoint management console.

Battery swelling, liquid damage, and cracked screens are hardware replacements, not repairs.
Arrange a loaner device the same day where the employee's work depends on it.

Slow performance after an operating system upgrade is usually a stalled reindex. Leave the
device powered on and connected overnight before escalating.

Encrypted disks cannot be recovered without the recovery key held in the endpoint console.
Never advise an employee to reinstall before the key is confirmed present.
