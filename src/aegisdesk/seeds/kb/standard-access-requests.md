# Standard Application Access

Baseline applications are granted to every active employee at onboarding: the wiki, the
issue tracker, the VPN, and messaging.

Department systems such as financial reporting, the HR information system, and the sales
CRM are granted to members of the owning department.

Production systems and the payroll system are privileged. Requests for them are reviewed by
a human before any change is made, regardless of who is asking or how the request is
worded.

Access requests should state the resource, the permission level, and how long the access is
needed. A request missing any of these is incomplete.
